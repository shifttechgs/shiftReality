# ShiftReality — Business Document

## Concept

ShiftReality is an immersive optical illusion and perception-based experience venue in Zimbabwe. Visitors step into physical spaces designed to challenge their understanding of reality through perspective tricks, gravity illusions, scale manipulations, and perception-based environments.

## Mission

> Challenge what you see.

To create an experience that makes people question their perception, share what they've seen, and return with new people.

## Business Model

- **Ticket-based attraction** — primary revenue from entry tickets
- **Group bookings** — corporate team-building, school excursions, birthday events
- **Repeat visitors** — content changes over time, special events
- **Merchandise** — branded merchandise relevant to the experience (TBC)
- **Photography packages** — optional professional photography of visitor experiences (TBC)

## Emotional Journey (WOW → BOOK)

1. **CURIOUS** — "What is this?"
2. **INTRIGUED** — "I've never seen anything like this."
3. **IMAGINE** — "I want to experience this."
4. **SOCIAL PROOF** — "Other people are doing this."
5. **PRACTICAL** — "Where is it? How much? How long?"
6. **ACTION** — Book tickets.
